About Me

I am Runnan Zhou (周润楠), a first-year M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at the Faculty of Information Engineering and Automation, Kunming University of Science and Technology (KUST), advised by Prof. Xinjie Yao. I received my B.Eng. degree in Urban Rail Transit from Southwest Jiaotong University (SWJTU) in 2020.

My interests lie in multimodal learning, machine learning and LLM-based agents. I am particularly curious about how a model can learn from heterogeneous data — vision, language and beyond — and how agentic systems can plan, use tools and act reliably in real tasks. I am always happy to discuss research and to collaborate.

Research Interests
